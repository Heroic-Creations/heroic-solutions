You've been cleared to work for Heroic Solutions. This briefing covers how to sign in, what tools you get, and the rules of employment.

Out of character

This is the plan for our campaign's website. It's still being built, so nothing is live yet. I'll send your login details when it's ready.

01Signing in

  • You'll get your own Heroic Solutions account from the DM.
  • Sign in, and you land on your My Apps page.
  • My Apps shows only the apps you have access to. Yours may differ from other players'.
  • The sign-in page is a real Microsoft sign-in page, dressed up as Heroic Solutions.

02Your apps

  • Heroic AIYour personal AI companion. See section 04.
  • Character creationBuild your character. Hover any option for a short explanation, or click it for the full details.
  • ShopSpend your credits on gear and cyberware.
  • MapThe campaign map of the city.
  • MailYour in-game inbox. Expect contacts, job offers and a lot of adverts.
  • Foundry VTTThe virtual tabletop we play on. Only switched on during sessions.

Some apps and features are locked until you pay for them. See section 03.

03Session fees

  • Each session, you can pay fees in in-game credits from your character's account.
  • Fees unlock things:
    • extra apps on your My Apps page
    • hidden features inside apps
    • upgrades for your AI companion, including a custom personality
  • Stop paying, and those things can be taken away again.
Out of character

No real money, ever. All fees are fake in-game credits. If anything ever asks you for a real card or payment, it isn't from me.

04Your AI companion

  • Everyone gets their own companion, with its own name, personality and memory of you.
  • Ask it about lore, gangs and local news.
  • It won't help with puzzles. Those are yours to crack.
  • Pay the right fee and you can customise its personality.
  • If it doesn't know something, it may get back to you later.
Notice: NetWatch monitors all traffic

Assume someone is always listening.

Out of character: I (the DM) can read your companion chats, and I can step in and reply as your companion at any time.

05Your account is live

  • Your credits, XP and items are tracked on the site and controlled by the DM.
  • What happens in the game can change them. This is Night City: watch your pockets.
  • Check your balance and inventory now and then.

06Ground rules

  • Don't share your login with anyone, including other players.
  • Use your account for the game only.
  • Rules explanations on the site are written in our own words. For the full rules, use the Core Rulebook.
  • Questions about the site? Ask the DM, not the AI.
